# NewJeans Signal Return As 4 Members With New Photos, Videos

## Summary
K-pop group NewJeans celebrates its fourth anniversary with a new film series featuring members Minji, Hanni, Haerin, and Hyein. This marks their first official content together in two years since their album *Supernatural*. The series, titled '2026 Summer of NewJeans,' includes nostalgic visuals and is described as a heartfelt gift for fans, following a legal battle that affected the group's lineup.

## Key Points
- NewJeans celebrates its fourth anniversary with a new film series.
- The series features all four active members for the first time in two years.
- The project follows a legal battle regarding member contracts.
